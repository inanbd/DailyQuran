Daily Quran is a quiet way to keep the Qur’an in your day. Read one ayah at a time with its translation, and pick up exactly where you left off — or choose a date and finish the whole Qur’an by then.

READ ONE AYAH AT A TIME
• The Arabic, a translation, or both, set for comfortable reading, in light or dark.
• Six translations: English (Saheeh International), Urdu (Maududi), Bengali (Muhiuddin Khan), French (Hamidullah), Spanish (García) and Russian (Kuliev). Add a second one under the first.
• Word by word: each Arabic word with its meaning.
• Hear a translation read aloud by your phone’s voice. The Arabic is never read by a machine voice — recitation has its own rules of tajwid.
• Swipe to turn the page, smooth as a gallery — or scroll straight through, surah after surah — and keep the ayat you love in Favourites.

THE QUR’AN PLANNER
• Choose when you want to finish — in a month, a year, or on any date — and see how many ayat a day that takes, and roughly how long.
• Your goal adjusts itself: read more one day and the days after get lighter; miss one and the rest is spread over the days left.
• Your plan keeps its own place, separate from your Daily Ayah.

REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOU
• Up to five reminders a day, for your Daily Ayah and for your plan. The later ones fall quiet once you have read.
• Nothing about your reading shows on the lock screen unless you ask for it.

READING TIME AND STREAKS
• Set a daily reading time, from 5 to 60 minutes. Only the time an ayah is on screen and you are with it is counted.
• A streak that only counts up: a word of congratulation every 3 days in a row, every tenth of the Qur’an, and when you read longer than the day before. A missed day starts it again without a word.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your place, plans, favourites and reading time stay on your phone, and reading works offline.
