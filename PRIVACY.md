# Daily Quran — Privacy Policy

*Effective 27 September 2026*

Daily Quran is a Qur'an reading app published by i9Tech. This policy covers
the app on Android (`com.i9tech.qurandaily`) and iOS.

## In short

Daily Quran does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has
no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and on
Android it does not even have permission to use the internet. Everything you
do in the app stays on your phone.

## What the app keeps on your phone

To work, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your
device:

- your place in the Qur'an, and which ayat you have read
- your reading plan and its progress
- how long you have read each day, and your streak
- your favourite ayat
- your settings: translation, text size, theme, reminder times and the like

None of this is sent anywhere. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the
app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

If you use your phone's own backup (Android Backup or iCloud), that backup may
include the app's data. Backups are made and kept by your phone's provider,
under your account and their privacy policy; the developer has no access to
them.

## Permissions

Every permission the app asks for serves reminders, and none is asked for
until you have chosen a reminder time. Declining any of them only makes
reminders less punctual; the rest of the app works the same.

- **Notifications** — to show your reminders.
- **Alarms and reminders** (exact alarms) — so a reminder arrives at the time
  you chose.
- **Run at startup** — to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
- **Vibration** — for the reminder itself.

The app may also send you to your phone's battery settings, where you can
choose to let it run unrestricted so your phone does not cancel reminders.
That is a setting you change yourself; the app asks for no permission to do
it.

## Text-to-speech

If you ask the app to read a translation aloud, the text is passed to the
text-to-speech engine installed on your phone (for example, Google's or
Samsung's). That engine is part of your phone, not of Daily Quran, and works
under its own provider's terms.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new
