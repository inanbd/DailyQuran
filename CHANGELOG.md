# Changelog

Each version's section is published as its GitHub Release notes.

## 1.2.0

**The Qur'an Planner** — finish the whole Qur'an by a day you choose.

- **Pick your finish date.** Finish in 1 month, in 1 year, or on any date you
  pick on a calendar. The planner tells you how many ayat to read each day.
- **Your goal adjusts itself.** Read more one day and the days after get
  lighter. Miss a day and the rest is spread over the days left. Your finish
  date stays the same.
- **Today's goal, first.** Your plan has its own daily reminder. Tap it to see
  today's goal — how much to read, how much is done, and where you will start —
  then tap **Continue reading**.
- **Continue reading** from the planner, the goal screen or the Progress tab
  takes you straight back into your plan.
- **Your plan and your Daily Ayah are kept separate.** Each has its own place
  and its own progress. Switch between them at the top of the Today screen with
  **Daily Ayah** / **My Plan**. Reading one never counts towards the other.
- **Start over whenever you need to.** *Plan again from today* keeps what you
  have read and spreads the rest from today. *Start from the first ayah* begins
  the plan again. *Stop my plan* turns it off. None of these touch your Daily
  Ayah.
- **Progress** now shows your plan (today's goal and the whole Qur'an) and your
  Daily Ayah side by side, and is where the planner lives.
- A plan you had already started keeps its progress when you update.
